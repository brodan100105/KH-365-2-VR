# Settings

Generated by `khvr_settings_doc` from `core/src/settings.cpp`; do not edit by hand.

Every option is on the in-headset VR panel (hold the left **Menu** button) and in
`/sdcard/Android/data/com.khdays.vr/files/khvr.ini`, which the app rewrites when you
close the panel. Each section below is an INI section; values out of range are clamped.

The panel groups the sections into pages (Screens, Hands & DS, View, Combat, Game), each a
list the thumbsticks scroll. Its first page, **Quick setup**, has profiles that set
everything a way of playing needs in one click (Giant screen, Both screens, DS in your
hands, DS + giant screen) and the options changed most.
